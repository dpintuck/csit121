JPG Format Example

River in a river

This image is a photo of a large black dog named River standing in the Severn River. Notice the ripples the dog makes in the water and the reflections along the shoreline.

Why JPG Format?

JPG is very popular across the web. This is due to its substantial quality but still relatively small file size. Most applications, browsers, and devices support JPG.
