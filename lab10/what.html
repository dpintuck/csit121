What is EPUB?

EPUB is a web standard developed by the International Digital Publishing Forum (IDPF). The EPUB format is the most widely used format for publishing digital media such as books, magazines, and comics. An EPUB file consists of a compressed ZIP file containing XHTML, CSS, images, and metadata.
