How is EPUB used?

EPUB is most used in the publishing of eBooks. Due to its wide adoption, publishers can provide a single EPUB file to retailers, where before they had to provide multiple file types. Governments such as the EU and Japan have moved to EPUB for official documents. In education, EPUB is being widely adopted for use with learning management systems.
