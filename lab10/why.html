Why is EPUB important?

The keywords to describe EPUB are accessibility and compatibility. It creates accessibility via its ability to adapt content to fit any screen size, and it works with screen readers. As for compatibility, it is compatible with most hardware. It is Windows, Apple, and even Amazon Kindle compatible.
